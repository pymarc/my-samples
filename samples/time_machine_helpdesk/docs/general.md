# ChronoShift X1 Time Machine – Documentation

---

## Overview

The ChronoShift X1 is an experimental time machine designed for short-range temporal displacement.

The machine allows controlled travel up to 72 hours into the past. Forward time travel is not supported.

The system operates using a stabilized temporal core, which generates a localized distortion in spacetime.

The device is intended for research and observation purposes only. Any attempt to alter historical events is strictly prohibited.

Only trained operators are allowed to use the system.

---

## Temporal Limits

The ChronoShift X1 supports travel only into the past.

Maximum backward travel range: 72 hours.

Minimum jump interval: 5 minutes.

Multiple consecutive jumps are allowed but increase the risk of temporal instability.

Travel beyond the allowed range will result in automatic system shutdown.

The system does not support travel into the future.

---

## Safety Guidelines

The machine must only be operated by certified personnel.

Transport of living organisms is strictly prohibited.

Operators must remain inside the protective chamber during activation.

Any red warning signal indicates critical instability in the temporal core.

In case of emergency, immediately initiate the shutdown protocol.

Unauthorized use of the machine may result in severe temporal anomalies.

---

## Startup Procedure

1. Ensure the temporal core is fully charged.  
2. Verify that all safety systems are operational.  
3. Enter the desired time offset using the control panel.  
4. Confirm operator identity via biometric scan.  
5. Press the activation switch and remain inside the chamber.  

The machine will begin temporal displacement within 5 seconds.

Do not exit the chamber during activation.

---

## Shutdown Procedure

### Normal shutdown

1. Disable the temporal core.  
2. Wait for stabilization (approx. 10 seconds).  
3. Exit the chamber.  

### Emergency shutdown

1. Press and hold the red emergency button for 3 seconds.  
2. The system will abort the temporal field.  
3. Remain inside the chamber until all indicators turn green.  

Emergency shutdown requires two authorized operators.

---

## Power System

The ChronoShift X1 is powered by a high-density quantum battery.

A full charge allows up to 3 temporal jumps.

Charging time: approximately 2 hours.

Low power levels will prevent activation of the machine.

The system does not use nuclear or chemical fuel sources.

---

## Maintenance

Routine maintenance must be performed every 30 operating hours.

The temporal core must be recalibrated weekly.

Only authorized technicians are allowed to service the machine.

Failure to perform maintenance may result in inaccurate time displacement.

System logs should be reviewed after each use.

---

## Troubleshooting

**Problem:** Machine does not start  
**Cause:** Insufficient power or failed biometric scan  
**Solution:** Recharge battery and verify operator identity  

**Problem:** Red warning light  
**Cause:** Temporal core instability  
**Solution:** Initiate emergency shutdown immediately  

**Problem:** Incorrect time displacement  
**Cause:** Calibration error  
**Solution:** Recalibrate temporal core  

Do not attempt to repair internal components manually.

---

## Temporal Paradox Policy

The ChronoShift X1 is designed for observation, not interaction.

Operators must avoid any actions that could alter historical events.

Direct interaction with past versions of oneself is strictly forbidden.

Any detected paradox risk will trigger automatic system lockdown.

Repeated violations may result in permanent access revocation.

---

## Emergency Protocols

In case of system failure:

1. Stay inside the chamber.  
2. Activate emergency shutdown.  
3. Wait for system stabilization.  

If temporal displacement fails, the system will attempt automatic return.

If automatic return fails, external recovery teams must be notified.

Never attempt to exit the chamber during instability.
